🔒 Privacy Policy — SalonBook

At SalonBook, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience, we collect the following types of information:

Personal Information: When you sign up using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.

Location Data: We request access to your precise and approximate location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).

2. Why We Request Permissions

We only ask for permissions that are essential for the app's functionality:

Location Permissions: We use your location data specifically to find and display salons near your current area and to provide accurate navigation to your chosen salon.

Notifications: We use push notifications to send you appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and important service updates.

Google Sign-In: We use this to provide a secure and quick way for you to manage your profile and booking history without needing to remember a new password.

3. Data Usage and Third Parties

Google Services: Our app uses Google Maps API to display salon locations and Google Sign-In for authentication. These services may collect data as governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Data Security: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

4. User Rights and Data Retention

You have the right to:

Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

Request the deletion of your account and personal data by contacting our support team.
