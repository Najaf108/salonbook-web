Privacy Policy

At SalonBook, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

📅 Effective Date: May 2026  ·  🇵🇰 SalonBook Pakistan
1
Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience, we collect the following types of information:

👤
Personal Information
When you sign up using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
📍
Location Data
We request access to your precise and approximate location — ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — to find salons near you.
2
Why We Request Permissions

We only ask for permissions that are essential for the app's functionality:

🗺️
Location Permissions
We use your location data specifically to find and display salons near your current area and to provide accurate navigation to your chosen salon.
🔔
Notifications
We send you appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and important service updates via push notifications.
🔑
Google Sign-In
We use Google Sign-In to provide a secure and quick way for you to manage your profile and booking history without needing to remember a new password.
3
Data Usage and Third Parties
🌐
Google Services
Our app uses Google Maps API to display salon locations and Google Sign-In for authentication. These services may collect data as governed by Google's own privacy policies.
🛡️
Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely.
4
User Rights and Data Retention

You have the right to:

  • ✓
    Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings without affecting other features of the app.
  • ✓
    Request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting our support team at any time.
  • ✓
    Access or update your personal information directly within the app through your profile settings.
